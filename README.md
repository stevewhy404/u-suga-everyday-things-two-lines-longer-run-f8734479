A make.fun entry: two lines that explain one everyday thing — a toaster — to a 10-year-old.

Line 1 explains the toaster through a vivid analogy; line 2 says why that analogy works, what it maps and what it leaves out.

Run `python3 main.py` and it writes the two lines to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
